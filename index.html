Controls

        

          
Mouse
Look

          
W A S D
Move (small area behind the firing line)

          
Shift
Sprint (hold)

          
Space
Crouch (hold)

          
F
Battle at night: flashlight on / off (bots spot a lit flashlight from far away)

          
X
Back to the firing line

          
Left button
Fire (hold for automatic)

          
Right button
Aim down sights (hold)

          
R
Reload

          
[ / ]
Previous / next sight (while not aiming)

          
1-9, 0
Select gun 1-10

          
Q / E
Lean left / right (hold; also the mouse side buttons)

          
- / +
Target closer / further (5, 10, 15, 20 m)

          
I
Inspect the weapon (shows its skin in Ultra)

          
K
Knife (Team Deathmatch, Capture the Flag, Bomb): lethal from behind, opens boarded doors in one hit

          
Caps Lock / G
Throw a frag grenade (Battle against bots, Team Deathmatch, Capture the Flag, Bomb): two a life, goes off 2.5 s after the throw; cover protects you

          
C
Range: clear all bullet holes and shot data. Battle: vault over low cover or climb onto a ledge (up to 1.5 m)

          
M
Target: dummy, paper (ring score), red dots (still), red dots (moving)

          
F3
Debug readout (Z zeroes its counters)

          
F4
Sight gallery (every sight side by side)

          
Esc
Pause (resume, change weapon, settings, skins, main menu)
