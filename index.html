You use the gun selected in Loadout. Player 2 plays with a gamepad (left stick move, right stick look, RT fire, LT aim, X reload, B crouch, L3 sprint, LB/RB lean) or the keyboard (I J K L move, arrow keys look, Enter fire, Right Shift aim, Backspace reload, / crouch). In split screen player 1 sprints with Left Shift.

      

      

      

        
Controls

        

          
Mouse
Look

          
W A S D
Move (small area behind the firing line)

          
Shift
Sprint (hold)

          
Space
Crouch (hold)

          
X
Back to the firing line

          
Left button
Fire (hold for automatic)

          
Right button
Aim down sights (hold)

          
R
Reload

          
[ / ]
Previous / next sight (while not aiming)

          
1-9, 0
Select gun 1-10

          
Q / E
Lean left / right (hold; also the mouse side buttons)

          
- / +
Target closer / further (5, 10, 15, 20 m)

          
I
Inspect the weapon (shows its skin in Ultra)

          
C
Clear all bullet holes and shot data (counters, score, plot)

          
M
Target: dummy, paper (ring score), red dots (still), red dots (moving)

          
F3
Debug readout (Z zeroes its counters)

          
F4
Sight gallery (every sight side by side)

          
Esc
Release the mouse and open this menu
