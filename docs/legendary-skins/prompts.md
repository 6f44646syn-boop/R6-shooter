# Recoil Range Legendary skin concepts

Nine original concept renders generated with the built-in ImageGen tool. Each image targets a different weapon from the Recoil Range roster.

Ubisoft’s event cosmetics informed the art direction. [M.U.T.E. Protocol](https://news.ubisoft.com/it-it/article/35Iym1Ib3oAMVvXQlFvZYf/rainbow-six-siege-mute-protocol-3-event-brings-back-arms-race-game-mode), [Wildcards Siege](https://news.ubisoft.com/pl-pl/article/73IFcGsQ8SOeVwvyqZRBa/rainbow-six-siege---wildcards-siege-event-launches-today), [Doktor’s Curse](https://www.ubisoft.com/en-us/news/ignt.64124/doktor-s-curse-is-back-until-november-2nd).

## Shared prompt

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset: one original Legendary weapon cosmetic concept for Recoil Range, inspired by the dimensional materials of Rainbow Six Siege's Masterpiece skins. Create one photorealistic PBR 3D studio product render of a virtual game prop. Wide landscape 3:2. Full silhouette, clean side profile, muzzle left, stock right, slight view from above so relief thickness catches light, centered with generous safe margins. Charcoal seamless background with subtle contact shadow, shaped softbox reflections and a delicate neutral rim light. Make the decoration look physically integrated into the exterior. Keep recognizable proportions and exposed dark hardware. Restrained cinematic effects near the finish only. Show a single gun with no hands, no firing, no people, no lettering, no logos, no interface, no watermark.

## Storm Kiln · F2

Design direction: Storm Kiln for the F2. F2 bullpup silhouette with a lacquered porcelain skin. Ivory glazed ceramic panels decorated with hand-painted deep cobalt storm clouds and breaking waves, fine blue brush edges, and selective silver-filled fracture seams where two glazes meet. One sculpted row of small cloud-scroll reliefs rises from the long side panel, fading into brushed blue-black metal. A tiny stainless inlaid moon is framed by the clouds. Keep the cobalt and ivory graphic readable at distance, with realistic milky glaze, minute crazing inside the porcelain, and cool silver specular highlights. No ice or snow.

## Abyssal Crown · G8A1

Design direction: Abyssal Crown for the G8A1. G8A1-style long support weapon silhouette. An antique deep-sea relic with sculpted nautilus whorls and layered ribs in dark oxidized bronze, selectively polished edges, rich dark teal translucent cabochons, sea-green patina in recesses, and a tiny antique depth dial ornament sealed beneath a domed glass detail on the broad side of the receiver. Engraved shells and tide lines curve from the dial across the body. Small pale green pinpricks trapped under the glass feel bioluminescent, with deep blue-green caustics across the bronze. Quiet and imposing, a museum-grade artifact recovered from a shipwreck. No dangling charm or literal crown sitting on top.

## Vesper Glass · C8-SFW

Design direction: Vesper Glass for the C8-SFW. C8-SFW carbine silhouette with a gothic stained-glass finish. Blackened pewter arches and finely sculpted oxidized tracery frame translucent deep garnet, violet, and smoky amber glass inlays along the side. A small circular rose-window medallion set into the receiver is the clear focal point. Build visible depth through beveled glass facets, dark lead seams, subtle micro-scratches and a little reflected red light on adjacent metal, with most surfaces remaining near black. A few tiny red refractions on the charcoal below the prop, never a background scene. Mood like a precious cathedral relic at dusk.

## Forgeheart · AK-12

Design direction: Forgeheart for the AK-12. AK-12 rifle silhouette. Thin layered plates of dark volcanic obsidian ceramic, sculpted satin black ridges, faint hammered copper and warm heat-blued titanium around the edges. A single narrow, irregular channel of rich molten orange under smoked translucent glass crosses the side from the front to the receiver, as if heat is buried below a cooling crust. Dark carbon fibre on the stock, brushed bronze around fasteners, two or three pin-sized embers suspended just above the hottest seam. Controlled contrast, almost black overall, with believable glass reflections and tiny dark bubbles suspended within the heated inlay.

## Amber Reliquary · MP5K

Design direction: Amber Reliquary for the MP5K. MP5K compact silhouette with a hand-finished fossil amber skin. A single large, gently convex honey-amber inlay follows the long side of the receiver. Suspended in its warm translucent depth is a small fossilized moth with spread wings, delicate vein patterns, a few preserved fern fragments, and tiny trapped air bubbles. The moth reads through the amber and remains dark and finely detailed. A branching fossil fern sculpted in low, overlapping relief in muted antique silver flows from the inset towards the foregrip and lower receiver. Surround the gold-honey stone with smooth satin blue-black metal and polished dark ebony on the stock, with controlled highlights, fine edge wear and engraved texture only around the fern. Emphasize the refractive glow of light traveling through real amber, the weight of the carved metal relief, and the recognizable silhouette of a close-to-stock MP5K. No neon, display graphics, loose charms, or extra objects.

## Jade Coil · T-5 SMG

Design direction: Jade Coil for the T-5 SMG. T-5 SMG silhouette with an antique East Asian jade sculpture finish. Blackened bronze body with a continuous carved nephrite serpent winding in restrained low relief along the side panels, a delicate head sculpted into the front exterior, dark forest-green scale edges catching light, and almost translucent honey-green jade underneath the larger polished scales. Thin warm gold inlay lines and smoky silk lacquer in recesses. Sculpted detail fits snugly to the prop and preserves its outline. Soft transmitted green light within the jade and a little naturally weathered bronze around high-contact points. Regal and ancient, rich green rather than neon.

## Velvet Ante · SMG-11

## Opal Overture · MP7

Design direction: Opal Overture for the MP7. MP7 silhouette with a dark graphite and opal art nouveau finish. Raised, flowing black chrome filigree wraps a few large mother-of-pearl panels, with fine nacre layers and vivid internal spectral flashes of peach, teal and violet at specific angles. Sculpted leaf-like relief and a single cabochon of natural black opal with a halo of fine polished inlay are the side focal point. Keep the rest of the shell restrained midnight charcoal with titanium edges. Light it like a hand-finished jewelry piece so the shifting rainbow hues appear inside the stone and the fine engraving is visible. No glitter particles or broad stripes.
