# Legendary weapon inspect prompts

These are production prompts for nine proposed Recoil Range skins. Sakura Gilt’s existing blossom-and-petal inspect is the reference for timing, material detail, and how the effect originates from the weapon. See the [concept lineup](lineup.png) and [original ImageGen prompts](prompts.md).

## Shared direction

Use the equipped weapon’s actual model and attachments. Preserve the silhouette and the finish shown in the reference render. In a roughly four-second first-person sequence, raise and turn the weapon from 0–0.8 s, reveal the material from 0.8–2.1 s, hit one signature moment around 2.4 s, and settle back to idle by 4.2 s. Keep the hands on believable anchors and leave the receiver readable through the effect. Make any light, particles, and reflections originate at the relevant surface. Let released particles drift independently as the gun moves.

Use the existing inspect input and cancel immediately on fire, aim, reload, or weapon swap. Reset animated pieces and effects on cancellation or return to idle. Keep the effect cosmetic. Use local meshes and bounded GPU particle batches with density scaled by graphics quality. Reserve full detail for the signature moment.

## Storm Kiln · F2

[Concept render](01-storm-kiln.png)

Tilt the ivory-and-cobalt body until a cool highlight reaches the silver repair seams. Let the raised porcelain waves lift in sequence from handguard to stock, following the painted strokes. At the peak, the tallest crest rolls over itself once and releases a short arc of clear droplets. Show the silver moon through the opening between the waves. As the weapon settles, the water beads slip back into the glaze and the relief returns to its resting profile.

## Abyssal Crown · G8A1

[Concept render](02-abyssal-crown.png)

Roll the bronze receiver toward the camera so the glass depth dial comes into view. Let its needle descend while faint teal caustics travel through the shell inlays. At the peak, the nautilus ribs open a fraction in sequence and release a sparse stream of small bubbles and dim bioluminescent specks from the seams. The needle settles, the ribs close, and the last bubbles rise clear as the weapon lowers.

## Vesper Glass · C8-SFW

[Concept render](03-vesper-glass.png)

Turn the dark pewter side into the light and let amber, garnet, and violet panes catch it one arch at a time. At the peak, rotate the inner rose-window ring a few degrees inside its frame, aligning the panes so a concentrated stained-glass reflection slides across the receiver. Let the pattern break apart as the angle changes, with a handful of tiny colored glints fading next to the glass. Seat the ring flush before returning to idle.

## Forgeheart · AK-12

[Concept render](04-forgeheart.png)

Raise the black ceramic body and let a dull red glow creep underneath the overlapping plates. The heat front traces the existing orange seam from the handguard into the receiver. At the peak, the plates lift just enough to expose a brighter molten channel under smoked glass. Release a few hot sparks along the split, with a small shimmer confined to the seam. The plates settle and the orange fades from yellow to deep red as the weapon lowers.

## Midnight Orrery · Scorpion EVO 3 A1

[Concept render](05-midnight-orrery.png)

Cant the midnight-blue body so the brass rings become visible beneath the glass. Give the inner planets distinct orbital speeds and keep the motion inside the domed window. At the peak, three beads align with the crescent, sending a warm specular sweep along the engraved constellation lines. The beads coast back into their resting arrangement as the highlight fades and the weapon lowers.

## Amber Reliquary · MP5K

[Concept render](06-amber-reliquary.png)

Roll the amber inset toward the light so parallax separates the preserved moth, fern fragments, and bubbles. Let the raised silver fern unfurl in short sections along the surrounding metal. At the peak, a golden highlight crosses the amber and briefly reveals the moth’s fine wing veins. Keep the moth fixed inside the resin. Release a few pollen-like motes from the silver fern tips, then fold the relief back as the light recedes.

## Jade Coil · T-5 SMG

[Concept render](07-jade-coil.png)

Reveal the carved serpent from tail to head as the weapon turns. Pass a green transmitted highlight under the overlapping jade scales, lifting each row subtly in its wake. At the peak, the head rises slightly from the bronze relief and its polished eye catches one sharp reflection. Let the scales settle in reverse order and the light sink back into the stone as the weapon lowers.

## Velvet Ante · SMG-11

[Concept render](08-velvet-ante.png)

Tip the oxblood enamel into the light so the gold Art Deco edges catch in sequence. Fan the three raised ivory cards from their shared pivot, keeping them anchored to the receiver. At the peak, spin the inset poker-chip medallion once and align its spade with the center card. Let the cards close one at a time while the chip settles back into its recess.

## Opal Overture · MP7

[Concept render](09-opal-overture.png)

Rotate the weapon slowly enough for the opal to shift from peach through teal to violet with the viewing angle. Let the raised black-chrome leaves open slightly around the central cabochon. At the peak, one prismatic highlight travels from the cabochon across the mother-of-pearl panels, with the color appearing inside the inlays. The leaves close as the weapon lowers and the opal returns to its resting reflection.
