# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Three groups, all confirmed: students playing in the browser on school laptops or Chromebooks (often on school Wi-Fi, sometimes together over a game code); gamers at home on PCs with mouse and keyboard or a controller; and anyone who finds the public site. Many play with friends, online or on one PC.

## Product Purpose

Recoil Range is a browser shooter built around real recoil. It is serious recoil practice (measured spray patterns for every gun and sight, a shooting range with dummies, targets and red dots) and a game with friends in equal measure: Battle vs bots, Gun Game, Endless, Battle Royale on Blacktide Island, Team Deathmatch, Capture the Flag and Bomb, offline against bots or online over a game code, casual or ranked. Success: players come back to train and to play, and the menus get them into the right game fast.

## Positioning

A static web page that plays like a full shooter with measured, per-gun recoil patterns, and needs no install: it runs from a link on a school laptop and still has Battle Royale, ranked play, online games through a small relay, packs, skins with 3D inspects and a season pass.

## Operating Context

Played in a browser tab, often on weak hardware and filtered networks; on strong PCs with the optional Enhanced graphics pack. Mouse and keyboard, controller, and trackpad all used. Several local accounts can share one PC. Online games go through a relay with a short game code, or same-network join codes. Ranked and online matches are fair-play locked.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Static site (index.html, js/, css/), no build step; Three.js vendored; optional downloadable packs (Enhanced graphics, Weapon Skins, audio).
- Graphics levels Low, Standard, High, Ultra (High and Ultra need the Enhanced pack to take effect).
- The menus come in two versions, a requirement from the user: a high-end version for High and Ultra, and a light low-end version for Low and Standard that stays fast on weak laptops.
- Terms in use: Home, Play, Pick game (Training, Ranked, Casual), Skins, Packs, Settings, Keybinds, Download, Season pass, Player stats, Challenges, points, rating and ranks (Copper, Bronze, Silver, Gold, Platinum, Diamond), Black Ice, Legendary, Epic.

## Brand Commitments

The name Recoil Range. No other visual commitment was made binding.

## Evidence on Hand

Real game screenshots of every map, time, weather and fog (data/menu/), skin art (data/skins/, skinpack/), the 3D gun models and skins rendered live. No testimonials, player counts or press exist; none may be invented.

## Product Principles

1. Get players into the right game fast: Play is one click away from Home.
2. Training and playing are equals; neither is buried under the other.
3. The light version must never cost a weak laptop frames; the high-end version earns its extra richness.
4. Show the real game (its guns, skins, maps), never stand-in art.
5. Fair play is visible: ranked is its own clearly marked choice.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Every menu reachable with mouse, keyboard and controller. Respect reduced motion.
